# AXEHOLD v1.4 — Identity & Game Feel

## Goal
Turn the production core into a game with a recognizable world, stronger mobile readability and a camp that feels like a place rather than a menu.

## Story foundation
Chapter I is built around **The Extinguishing**. A network of ancient Hearths once held the Dark back. One by one they went silent. The player's Last Hearth is the final known flame.

The Wanderer initially believes the Guardians caused the disaster. Relics progressively reveal a different truth: Guardians originally protected the Hearth network. By the third relic the player learns that defending one flame forever is not enough.

Story delivery stays environmental and compact:
- camp state;
- map taglines;
- relic clues;
- ruins and activities;
- boss identities;
- short non-blocking copy.

## Hub redesign
The camp becomes the persistent emotional center of the game.
- larger single-screen living composition;
- warm Hearth glow;
- visible Wanderer and residents;
- progression-dependent forge, arsenal rack, palisade, watchtower and stronghold;
- hotspots remain the navigation model;
- chapter whisper and next-expedition card replace empty menu space.

## Arsenal redesign
The arsenal now presents the selected weapon as a gameplay identity.
- animated weapon preview;
- readable role copy;
- four visual meters;
- compact unlock cards;
- no emoji dependency in production labels.

## World map redesign
The biome selector becomes a vertical route from the Last Hearth into the Dark.
- connected biome nodes;
- selected route highlight;
- mastery and relic state on nodes;
- biome lore and gameplay rule shown beneath the map.

## Trophy redesign
Boss relics become physical objects on pedestals.
- three dedicated alcoves;
- mastery shown per biome;
- each obtained relic reveals a short clue about the Extinguishing;
- daily tasks remain separate from the trophy hall.

## Gameplay readability
Frost Hollow was visually too washed out on phone screens. v1.4 deepens biome base palettes, reduces frost overlay brightness and strengthens cracks and foreground separation.

## Last Hearth identity
The Hearth receives a larger warm world-space glow so it functions simultaneously as:
- home;
- navigation beacon;
- safety cue;
- story symbol.

## Definition of done
v1.4 passes when:
- camp reads as a place before it reads as UI;
- map reads as a route, not a list;
- weapon choice is understandable visually;
- trophies feel collectible and persistent;
- Frost Hollow keeps foreground objects readable;
- the Last Hearth is immediately recognizable at gameplay scale;
- all previous gameplay regressions still pass.
