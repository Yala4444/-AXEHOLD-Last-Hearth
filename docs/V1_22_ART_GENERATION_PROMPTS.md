# v1.22 — Production Art Prompt Set

Все изображения созданы встроенным ImageGen с визуальным концептом AXEHOLD v1.21 в роли style reference.

## Общая основа

> Use case: stylized-concept. Production cutout for a top-down 2D mobile action roguelite. Premium hand-painted dark folk fantasy game art matching the AXEHOLD reference: realistic worn materials, painterly edges, intricate but readable mobile silhouette, elevated 3/4 top-down gameplay perspective facing down-right. Exactly one complete isolated subject centered with generous padding and a compact contact shadow. Genuinely transparent background with clean alpha. No environment, frame, UI, text, watermark, sprite-sheet grid, cropped body, pixel art, cartoon proportions or bright saturated colors.

## Персонажи

- **Wanderer:** sturdy hooded hero in battered layered iron armor and dark oxblood torn cloak; face hidden in shadow, one amber eye, glowing Last Hearth chest rune, leather straps and satchel; empty hands because weapons render separately.
- **Root Husk:** small corrupted former human overtaken by thorny roots, broken bark plates and ragged black-brown cloth; hunched stance, thin limbs, ember eyes and a subtle red corruption core.
- **Briar Hound:** low forward-leaning root-and-hide predator with very long forelimbs, root claws, narrow bone mask and antler splinters; unmistakably fast.
- **Ironroot Ravager:** broad asymmetric brute fused with roots, salvaged iron shoulder plates, stone fists, short branch horns and a molten corruption core.
- **Hollow Seer:** unnaturally tall floating revenant with hanging root-cloth strips, branch crown, elongated twig fingers and a pale ritual mask carrying one vertical red mark.
- **Oathstone Bulwark:** squat defensive construct of carved standing stones, dark roots, oxidized iron bands, slab torso and a tiny amber rune core.
- **Forest Guardian:** enormous asymmetric tree colossus of tangled roots, split bark and embedded rune stones; irregular antlers, small conifers, deer-skull mask with one amber eye, moss, fungi, lichen, muted red leaves and a living green-amber heart.

## Мир

- **Last Hearth:** low circular rune-stone fire pit intertwined with roots, powerful amber flame, three uneven carved guardian stones, iron offering bowl and weathered red cloth strips.
- **Ancient Sentinel Tree:** massive twisted split tree with exposed roots, broken arching limbs, hanging moss, shelf mushrooms, muted red leaves and an old rune stone fused into its bark.
- **Harvest Tree:** smaller chop-able twisted woodland tree with a sturdy trunk, sparse dark foliage, exposed roots, mushrooms and a few red leaves.
- **Stone Deposit:** four irregular dark slate boulders with chipped planes, threading roots, moss and one muted red leaf.
- **Ore Deposit:** broken dark rune stone pierced by three restrained smoky-violet crystals, roots and moss, with a faint lavender inner glow.

## Техническая обработка

Выбранные PNG обрезаются по альфа-границе, уменьшаются до 320–768 px по длинной стороне и сохраняются в lossless WebP. В Godot они отображаются значительно меньше исходного разрешения, что сохраняет мелкие материалы и чистый силуэт на Retina-экранах.
